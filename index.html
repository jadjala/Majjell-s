Our Story

A neighborhood table where everyone's a regular.

Majjells started with a simple idea: make the food we'd want to feed our own family, and make room for everyone at the table. We're fancy enough for a celebration, easygoing enough for a Tuesday.

Our kitchen runs all day — sweet snacks and shakes in the afternoon, sizzling plates and rice meals by dinner, and a tall, generous halo-halo whenever the day calls for one. Pastries and custom cakes are baked in-house, and we happily cook for a crowd with bulk orders.
